# Products

## Overview

The product module is the central part of the TissuLuxe e-commerce experience. It allows textile products to be displayed clearly and professionally.

---

## Product Information

Each product can include:

- Product name
- Product image
- Short description
- Detailed description
- Category
- Price
- Availability status
- Product reference

---

## Product Categories

TissuLuxe can organize textile products by category, such as:

- Luxury fabrics
- Traditional fabrics
- Modern fabrics
- Cotton
- Silk
- Linen
- Wool
- Decorative textiles
- Special collections

---

## Product Card Design

A professional product card should include:

- High-quality image
- Clear product name
- Short description
- Price display
- Call-to-action button
- Availability indicator

---

## Product Detail Page

A product detail page should provide:

- Large product image
- Full product description
- Price
- Category
- Related products
- Contact or order action

---

## Best Practices

- Use consistent image sizes.
- Keep product descriptions clear.
- Avoid low-quality images.
- Organize products by category.
- Make the design responsive.
- Highlight featured products on the homepage.
