# Roadmap

## Overview

This roadmap describes the planned evolution of TissuLuxe as a professional textile e-commerce platform.

---

## Version 1.0 – Core Storefront

Main objectives:

- Homepage
- Product catalog
- Product details
- Categories
- Contact section
- Responsive design
- Basic admin dashboard

---

## Version 2.0 – Store Management

Main objectives:

- Product management
- Category management
- Order management
- Customer management
- Improved admin panel
- Better image management
- Database improvements

---

## Version 3.0 – Complete E-Commerce

Main objectives:

- Shopping cart
- Checkout process
- Customer accounts
- Payment integration
- Delivery tracking
- Product reviews
- Promotions and discounts

---

## Long-Term Vision

TissuLuxe aims to become a complete and professional textile e-commerce solution with a luxury visual identity and a smooth customer experience.
